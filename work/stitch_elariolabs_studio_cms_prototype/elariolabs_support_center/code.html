04 // Principles

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

Engineering answers to the most common questions surrounding data models and licensing.

Zero analytics SDKs • Zero tracking beacons • Zero external user DBs
All ElarioLabs applications operate on an absolute zero-telemetry doctrine. We do not integrate third-party metric trackers, session recorders, or advertising SDKs. Data generated inside our applications lives locally on your physical drive inside an encrypted SQLite database. When synchronization is enabled, records flow exclusively through your private Apple CloudKit or user-configured WebDAV instances.
The compilation flags, engine performance, and feature parity between Direct DMG and Mac App Store releases are 100% equivalent. The Direct DMG distributions utilize Sparkle with EdDSA signatures for instantaneous bug patch delivery without App Store review latency, and allow CLI binary integration inside your system /usr/local/bin directory.
When you purchase any ElarioLabs application, you receive a perpetual, non-revocable cryptographic license for that major revision cycle (e.g., version 2.x). Your purchase includes free point updates and minor feature refinements for the lifetime of that major branch. No ongoing subscriptions, no remote kill-switches, and complete offline authorization capabilities.
Because our applications adhere to open standards, migration is as simple as copying your local container folder located at ~/Library/Application Support/ElarioLabs/[AppName]. Alternatively, use the built-in "Export Sovereign Vault" tool located in Preferences > Storage to produce an atomic portable bundle containing schema, media assets, and WAL logs.
To help our engineers isolate anomalies rapidly, provide: (1) macOS version and hardware configuration, (2) the exact build number, (3) reproducible trigger steps, and (4) the sanitized diagnostic log generated via Help > Export Diagnostics Packet. These diagnostic packets automatically scrub private personal content and key data.
