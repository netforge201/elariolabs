#ISS-4091

macOS Sonoma 14.5 Stage Manager Dimmer Flickering

Workaround Available

When Stage Manager switches active application sets while Focusly Glaze Dimmer is above 60% opacity, the Metal compositing pipeline occasionally renders a 1-frame strobe on secondary monitors.

Immediate Fix: Open Preferences > Display Glaze > check “Disable Hardware Compositing Layer on Secondary Displays” until 2.3.1 patch applies.
Reported: 3d ago Affects macOS 14.5

#ISS-4084

CoreAudio buffer underrun when connecting AirPods Max over USB-C

Fixed in v2.3.1-beta

Hot-plugging high-res lossless audio over direct USB-C cables caused our SIMD binaural frequency generator to misread the hardware clock sync, leading to faint micro-clicks in the left ear channel.

Resolution: NEON SIMD audio generator re-anchored to instantaneous 48kHz clock synchronizer. Included in build 2.3.1-beta (Build 412).
Resolved: Beta 412 CoreAudio Unit

Knowledge Clarification

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

Focusly is engineered around biological rhythms, silent data sovereignty, and zero-telemetry architecture. Here is how our execution differs from standard productivity software.

encrypted The Zero-Surveillance Promise

We operate zero central databases. Your focus telemetry, session logs, and tags are physically stored on your local disk in an encrypted SQLite vault and synced directly via Apple CloudKit.

Read ElarioLabs Zero-Surveillance Charter open_in_new

Standard Pomodoro software imposes an arbitrary 25-minute clock regardless of your cognitive state, often triggering micro-anxiety and interrupting deep problem-solving flow. Focusly implements Biological Pacing based on 90-minute ultradian rhythm curves. Rather than ticking countdowns, it generates low-distortion 40Hz binaural waves that gently anchor beta/gamma brainwave coherence, notifying you of natural replenishment windows through soft physical cues rather than abrasive alarms.

Absolutely zero. Focusly contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no behavioral crash reporters (e.g., Sentry, Firebase), and no tracking pixels. All sound generators run purely client-side through local CoreAudio graphs. Session history is encrypted at rest using Apple Keychain hardware keys in a local SQLite file. We cannot view, monetize, or decrypt your productivity timestamps under any circumstance.

Navigate to Focusly Preferences > Data Vault. You can trigger an instant, raw export in three formats:

  • JSON: Structured schema including session duration, heart rate variability markers (if Apple Watch connected), and tags.
  • CSV: Clean tabular format ready for import into spreadsheets, Obsidian, or Notion.
  • Markdown: Clean human-readable journal entries partitioned by project tags.
